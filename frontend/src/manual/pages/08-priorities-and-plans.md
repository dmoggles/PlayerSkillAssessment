Open **Development** and choose the player. The coach assessment needs to be complete first.

## Choosing priorities

TapLine IDP suggests the **three most urgent skills** for the player. Urgency is higher when:

- the skill is rated low (your rating and the player's, averaged, if they've rated themselves);
- it matters a lot for the player's position (and secondary position, if they have one);
- it's a foundation for other skills that are also weak.

Press a skill's urgency score to see how it was worked out. Keep the suggestions, or choose other skills from the ranked list, and add a note on why if you like. A priority's note is shown in the player's report, so write it for them. Each priority is labelled *Suggested*, *Carried over* from the last cycle, or *Override* when you chose differently.

Press **Save priorities**. Nothing is decided automatically: the suggestions are a starting point.

## The four-week plan

Press **Generate plan**. For each priority, the plan picks:

- **At training:** a drill at the right level for the player's rating.
- **At home:** a drill they can do alone or with a parent, where the library has one.

Each drill steps up after two weeks: *Weeks 1–2* use one version, *Weeks 3–4* a harder one. Each pick says why it was chosen.

![A four-week plan with a training drill and a home drill](/landing/plan.jpg)

- **Change** a drill: choose from the planner's ranking for that skill, or any drill in the library, and its starting version. **Reset to planner's choice** undoes it.
- **Regenerate** after changing the priorities.
- **Generate plans for the squad** makes plans for every player with saved priorities in one go.

## Development cycles

A plan runs for one **cycle**. When it's done, press **Start cycle 2**. A quick **check-in** asks how each focus skill went (better, same or worse, with an optional note); then you choose the next priorities. You can skip the check-in. Earlier cycles are kept as history, and check-ins never change the period's ratings.

## Drills for each priority

Under each priority, **Drills** lists matching drills from the library, at the version that suits the player's level. Open one to see it in full. See [The drill library](/manual/drill-library).
