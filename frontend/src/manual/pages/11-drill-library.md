The drill library is shared by every team. Open it from **Settings → Drill library**, or from a priority in **Development**.

## Finding a drill

Search by name or skill, or filter by area, skill, part of the session (warm-up, technical, opposed, small-sided game or cool-down), format (individual, small group, unit or whole team), intensity and the equipment you have. **Can be done at home** shows drills a player can do alone or with a parent; each one says how to do it indoors safely.

## Inside a drill

- **Variations:** each drill has a base version, an easier one and harder ones, each with the levels (1 to 5) it suits. Choose one, and the setup, instructions and coaching points change to match; changed sections are marked. Some drills work differently:
  - drills marked **Any focus** train no particular skill, such as a general warm-up, so choose the version that suits the group;
  - set programmes, like the FIFA 11+ warm-ups, start everyone at the first version, and players move up one at a time.
- **Diagram:** press **Play** to watch the drill move step by step, or use the arrows to step through it.
- **Videos:** some drills have a short video. Nothing loads until you press **Play video**.
- **Like** or **Dislike** a drill. Disliked drills move to the end of your list and aren't suggested to you; votes are anonymous.

## Your team's drills

Press **Add a drill** to write up a drill you already run. Only your team's coaches see it, marked *Your team*, and the planner can choose it for sessions and players' plans like any library drill. **Only my team's drills** under Filters shows just these.

- **What it needs:** a name, the part of the session, how many players, the age groups, the minutes, the set-up and the steps. Coaching points and equipment are optional.
- **Skills it trains:** choose at least one it mainly trains (Main), plus any it helps a little (Partial). Warm-ups and cool-downs needn't train a skill.
- **Easier and harder versions** are optional. Level groups and players' plans use them: the easier version for players rated 1–2, the harder for 4–5.
- **No diagram:** your own drills don't have animated diagrams. Describe the set-up clearly, and add a YouTube or Vimeo link if you have a video of it.

**Edit** changes it everywhere, including sessions and plans that use it. **Delete** takes it out of the library; sessions and plans that already use it keep it.

### Your own version of a library drill

Want to run a library drill your way? Press **Make our own version** on it. You start from a copy of its words (set-up, steps, coaching points, and the easier and harder versions nearest the base) and change anything. Your version replaces the library drill for your team: the library drill is hidden from your team's library, and sessions and plans choose yours. Other teams still see the library drill.

Your version has only what you write, with no diagrams or videos from the library drill. It links to the library drill, so its diagrams are a click away. **Unlink** keeps both as separate drills; **Use the library version instead** deletes yours and brings the library drill back. Sessions and plans that already use either drill keep it.

### Sharing it with everyone

**Submit to the shared library** offers your drill to every coach. A site admin reviews it. If it's accepted, we write it up with diagrams before it appears in the library, without naming you or your team; your drill shows where it stands. Only submit a drill that's yours, or your club's, to share. Once it's in the library, your drill becomes your team's version of it, so you don't see both.

## Suggesting a video

Know a good video for a drill? Press **Suggest a video** on the drill, paste a YouTube or Vimeo link, and say why it's a good fit. A site admin checks every suggestion before anyone else sees it, and you can follow yours in **Settings → Account**.

If it's approved, the video is shown to everyone who uses TapLine IDP, including players through shared reports. So:

- only suggest a video that is public, or that you have the owner's permission to share;
- if it shows players from your club, check with your club's welfare officer first;
- don't suggest links that were shared with you privately, such as unlisted videos or links from a club or parents' group.

## Heading drills

Heading drills are for U12 and older only, and follow the FA's guidance on how many headers players take in training.
