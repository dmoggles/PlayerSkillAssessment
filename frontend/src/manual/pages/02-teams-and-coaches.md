A team is a squad with its own players, periods and skill matrix. You can belong to several teams, and switch between them at the top of any area.

## Owners and coaches

Every team member is either an **owner** or a **coach**.

- **Coaches** assess players, choose priorities, make plans and share reports. They can add, rename and archive players, and add periods.
- **Owners** can do all that, and also manage the team in **Settings → Team access**: rename it, invite and remove coaches, change roles, edit the skill matrix, delete periods and players, export a player's data, and delete the team.

A team always has at least one owner. To step down or leave, make another member an owner first.

## Inviting a coach

1. In **Settings → Team access**, type the coach's email address and press **Invite coach**.
2. We email them an invitation. You also see the link, to copy and send yourself (for example by WhatsApp) if the email doesn't arrive.

The invitation works once, for that email address, within 7 days. If the coach already has an account, they sign in and join the team from the link.

## Changing roles and removing coaches

In the **Members** list, use **Make owner** or **Make coach** to change a role, and **Remove** to take someone out of the team. A coach can leave a team themselves from **Settings → Team membership**.

## Team settings

- **Players:** sets the pronouns used in skill descriptions: girls (she), boys (he) or mixed (they), so the level descriptions read naturally for your squad.
- **Allow player self-assessment:** turns on links for players to rate themselves. See [Player self-assessment](/manual/self-assessment).

## Activity log

Open **Activity** in **Settings → Team access** to see who did what and when: invitations, role changes, deleted periods, shared reports and more.

## Deleting a team

**Delete team** permanently removes the team with all its players, periods, assessments, priorities and plans, and everyone's access to it. You type the team's name to confirm. It can't be undone, so export anything you want to keep first.
