---
title: Player self-assessment
summary: Send players a private link to rate themselves, and compare their view with yours.
---
Players can rate themselves on the same skills, from a link you send them. Their view sits beside yours, which makes a good start to a conversation about what to work on.

## Turning it on

An owner ticks **Allow player self-assessment** in **Settings → Team access**.

## Sending links

For the whole squad, open **Settings → Player self-assessment** and press **Create links**. For one player, use **Create link** at the bottom of their **Assessment**.

- Each link is for one player and the active period. It works **once**, for **7 days**.
- Copy links straight away: they're shown only once. **Copy all** copies every new link at once, ready to paste into a team chat.
- **Revoke** stops a link working. **New link** replaces it.

The list shows where each player is: not sent, sent, opened, submitted or expired.

## What the player sees

The link opens a page with their name and team, no sign-in needed. They choose outfield or goalkeeper, rate each skill from 1 to 5 (or *I don't know*), and submit. They can't see your ratings or notes.

## Using the results

- **Player Data → Comparison** shows your rating and theirs side by side, skill by skill.
- **Player Data → Summary** points out the biggest disagreements, especially on foundation skills.
- When suggesting priorities, the player's rating and yours are averaged.

> **Tip:** A player who rates themselves higher than you do may need to see what the next level looks like. One who rates themselves lower may need confidence more than coaching.
