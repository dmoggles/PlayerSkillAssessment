## Periods

A **period** is a stretch of the season you assess in, such as *Autumn 2026* or *Spring 2027*. Each player gets one coach assessment per period, and their priorities and plans belong to it too. Comparing periods shows each player's progress.

One period is **active**: it's the one new self-assessment links are made for, and the one the app opens on. Use **Set active** to change it.

To add a period, type its name under **Settings → Periods** and press **Add period**. Two options:

- **This period starts a new season:** every player moves up one age group. Before the period is created you see the list, to adjust anyone who is staying down or playing up.
- **Start from the last period's ratings:** each rating is copied across and marked *Carried from last period* until you review it. Use this when not much has changed and you'd rather adjust than start from scratch.

Owners can delete a period. That permanently deletes every assessment, self-assessment and priority in it.

## The squad

Under **Settings → Squad**:

- **Add player:** type the name and press the button.
- **Playing group:** choose each player's age group for the selected period. Ratings are judged against what's expected at that age, so moving a player to another group is marked in their progress.
- **Rename** a player at any time.
- **Archive** a player who has left. Their history stays, read-only, and they drop out of the lists. **Restore** brings them back.
- **Delete…** (owners, archived players only) removes a player and everything recorded about them, permanently. You type their name to confirm.
- **Export data** (owners) downloads everything recorded about a player, as a file. That's what to send if a player or parent asks for a copy of their information.

> **Tip:** We only ask for a player's name. Don't add dates of birth, contact details or medical information to names or notes.
