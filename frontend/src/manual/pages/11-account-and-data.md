## Your account

In **Settings → Account**:

- **Change password:** at least 12 characters.
- **Sign out** of this device.
- **Delete account…:** see below.

Forgotten your password? Use **Forgot password** on the [sign-in page](/sign-in), and we'll email you a link to set a new one.

## Deleting your account

**Delete account…** asks for your password, then deletes your account straight away. Your teams keep the players, assessments and plans you recorded, with your email taken off them. If you're a team's only owner, make another coach an owner, or delete the team, first.

## Players' information

The club decides what's kept about its players, and TapLine IDP holds it on the club's behalf. We only ask for a player's name, team and age group: never dates of birth, addresses, contact details or photos.

If a player or parent asks:

- **for a copy of their information:** an owner uses **Export data** in **Settings → Squad**, and sends them the file;
- **for it to be deleted:** an owner archives the player, then uses **Delete…**. Everything recorded about them goes straight away, and from our backups within about three months.

The [privacy notice](/privacy) has the full details, and [For players and parents](/privacy/players) explains it in plain words.
