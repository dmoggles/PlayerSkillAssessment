---
title: Reports for players and parents
summary: Share a player's report and home drills through a private link.
---
Each player has a report for each period, in **Player Data → Report**. It shows:

- a message from you;
- their focus areas for the next period, with any note you added to a priority, and how last period's went;
- how their ratings have moved in each area, period by period;
- their home drills from the plan, each with its easier and harder version.

Notes you wrote on the assessment, and the player's own ratings, are never included.

## Writing the message

Type a few words of encouragement or context under **Message to the player**, and save. It appears at the top of the report.

## Sharing it

Press **Create link** and copy it: it's shown only once. Anyone with the link can open the report and its drills without signing in, so send it only to the player or their parents.

- The link lasts until **a week after the plan ends** (30 days if there's no plan).
- **Extend** keeps the same link working for 1, 2 or 4 more weeks.
- **Revoke** stops it working at once. **New link** replaces it.
- You can see when it was shared, when it expires, and whether it's been opened.

**Print or save as PDF** gives you a copy to hand out instead.

> **Tip:** Players and parents can read [For players and parents](/privacy/players), which explains in plain words what's recorded and who can see it.
