---
title: The skill matrix
summary: The skills your team is rated on, and how to change them.
---
The **skill matrix** is the list of skills your team rates, grouped into sections (such as technical, tactical and goalkeeper skills), with what levels 1, 3 and 5 look like for each.

Every team starts from the built-in matrix. Owners can adapt it in **Settings → Skill matrix → Edit skill matrix**.

## What you can change

- **Rename** a skill or a section, and rewrite its level descriptions. Use the placeholders offered so the wording follows the team's *Players* setting (she, he or they).
- **Add** a skill or a section.
- **Retire** a skill: it won't be rated in new periods. Past ratings are kept.
- **Importance by position:** how much each skill matters for each position. This drives which priorities are suggested.
- **Foundation for:** marks a skill that other skills build on. A weak foundation skill is suggested as a priority sooner.
- **Tags:** link the skill to drills. Choose one *Main* tag, and *Partial* tags for anything else it clearly involves.

## Drafts and publishing

Changes are saved as a draft as you go; nobody else sees them until you publish. Press **Review and publish** to see what changed, fix anything marked, and choose where the new version applies:

- **New periods only**, or
- **New periods and the current period**, if nobody has been assessed in it yet.

> **Tip:** Adding skills is safe. Changing or retiring skills makes ratings harder to compare across periods, so changed skills are marked in each player's progress. You're asked to confirm you understand this before publishing.
