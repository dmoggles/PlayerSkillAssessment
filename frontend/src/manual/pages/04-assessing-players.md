---
title: Assessing players
summary: The 1 to 5 scale, positions, notes and revision history.
---
Open **Assessment**, choose the player and period, and rate each skill.

## The scale

Every skill is rated from **1 to 5**, against what's expected at the player's age group:

- **1 Developing:** not yet at the level expected for their age.
- **3 Achieving:** at the level expected.
- **5 Excelling:** beyond it.
- **2 and 4** sit in between.

Each skill describes what 1, 3 and 5 look like, so coaches rate the same way. Press a level to choose it; press it again to clear it.

## Positions

Choose the player's **primary position**. Goalkeepers are rated on goalkeeper skills; everyone else on outfield skills. If the player also plays a **secondary position**, choose it and how often (rarely, sometimes or often): it changes which skills matter most when suggesting priorities.

Changing between goalkeeper and outfield clears the ratings, since the skills are different.

## Notes

- **Overall note:** anything to remember about this assessment.
- **Add note** under a skill: a note on that rating.

Notes are for coaches only. They're never shown to players or parents.

## Saving

Press **Save assessment**. Priorities need the coach assessment to be complete: every skill rated.

Every save is kept. Open **Revision history** under the form to see each version, who saved it, and what changed.

## Carried ratings

If the period started from the last period's ratings, carried ones are marked *Carried from last period*. Change the rating, or press **Keep** if it still holds; **Mark all as reviewed** keeps them all.

## Archived players

An archived player's assessments open read-only. Restore the player in **Settings → Squad** to change them.
