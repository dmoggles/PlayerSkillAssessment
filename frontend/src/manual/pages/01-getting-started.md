TapLine IDP helps you build an individual development plan for each player: you rate their skills, agree what they should work on next, and give them drills to practise at training and at home.

## Joining

TapLine IDP is invitation-only for now. You join in one of two ways:

- **A new club:** request access on the [home page](/). We'll email you an invitation to create your account, then you set up your own team.
- **An existing team:** a team owner invites you from **Settings → Team access**. The invitation email has a link that works for 7 days; open it, choose a password, and you're in the team.

Invitation links work once, for the email address they were sent to.

## Setting up your team

Everything is set up in **Settings**:

1. **Create the team.** Under **Teams**, type the team's name and press **Add team**. You become its owner.
2. **Add a period.** Under **Periods**, add one, such as *Autumn 2026*. Assessments, priorities and plans all belong to a period. See [Players and periods](/manual/players-and-periods).
3. **Add your players.** Under **Squad**, type each name and press **Add player**. Set each player's playing group (U9, U10 and so on) for the period: ratings are judged against what's expected at that age.
4. **Invite your coaches**, if you have any. See [Teams and coaches](/manual/teams-and-coaches).

## Your first assessment

1. Open **Assessment** and choose the player.
2. Set their primary position, then rate each skill from 1 to 5. See [Assessing players](/manual/assessing-players).
3. Press **Save assessment**.
4. Open **Development** to choose their priorities and make a plan. See [Priorities and plans](/manual/priorities-and-plans).

> **Tip:** On a phone, add TapLine IDP to your home screen (in your browser's Share or menu: *Add to Home Screen*). It then opens like an app, straight into your workspace.

## The six areas

- **Assessment:** rate a player for the period.
- **Player Data:** one player's results, profile, progress and report.
- **Team Data:** the whole squad at a glance.
- **Development:** priorities, drills and the player's plan.
- **Sessions:** team training sessions planned around what your players are working on.
- **Settings:** teams, periods, players, coaches, the skill matrix and your account.

The team, period and player you choose at the top stay chosen as you move between areas.
